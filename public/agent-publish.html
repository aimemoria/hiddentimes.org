Publish a story

Before publishing, check:
  • The topic is not already in the list of stories below.
  • Every fact comes from a credible source, and the sources are listed.
  • Documented facts are kept apart from theories and speculation.
  • No invented quotations or details.
  • The cover image may legally be reused (public domain, Creative Commons, or your own illustration), and its credit is filled in.

A published story needs: a title (10+ characters), an author, a category, a short description (40 to 300 characters), at least 300 words, at least one source, a cover image with a description and a credit. A draft only needs a title and some text.
